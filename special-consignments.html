Our Services / Special Consignments

Bulk & Special Consignments

Greater control for consignments beyond ordinary courier handling.

Freight capacity with safe-hand accountability

Some official movements require the capacity of freight logistics together with the accountability of a safe-hand operation.

Sovereignty Logistics can coordinate bulk or special consignments requiring planned acceptance, packaging and seal checks, transfer oversight, stakeholder liaison and documented final receipt.

Service capabilities

  • Multi-package and bulk movements
  • Controlled acceptance procedures
  • Packaging and seal coordination
  • Piece-count and consignment reconciliation
  • Loading and unloading oversight
  • Airport transfer coordination
  • Approved screening coordination
  • Route disruption planning
  • Final handover documentation

Screening arrangements

Screening arrangements are considered during planning and conducted only under the agreed authority, instructions and operating conditions applicable to the assignment. Where a proposed screening process is not authorised, the movement is paused while an approved alternative, reroute or return arrangement is established.
