Our Process

Our Process

A controlled process from first instruction to confirmed completion.

1

Confidential enquiry

The client submits a high-level description of the requirement without including sensitive operational information.

2

Authority and client verification

We confirm the organisation, responsible contact, authority to request the service and appropriate communication pathway.

3

Secure onboarding

Approved users receive access to the secure portal, where detailed instructions and supporting documents can be exchanged.

4

Operational assessment

We assess the route, timeframe, consignment profile, interfaces, documentation requirements and foreseeable operational risks.

5

Service plan and proposal

The client receives a defined scope covering responsibilities, inclusions, exclusions, commercial terms and required approvals.

6

Instruction and mobilisation

Following acceptance, the assignment is scheduled and the relevant participants are briefed.

7

Assignment delivery

The movement is conducted in accordance with approved instructions, with agreed status updates and exception escalation.

8

Handover and closeout

Final receipt is recorded and applicable assignment records are returned or retained according to the agreed requirements.
