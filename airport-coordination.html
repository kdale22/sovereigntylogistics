Our Services / Aviation Coordination

Airport & Airside Coordination

Planned coordination at critical airport interfaces.

Coordination where multiple systems meet

Airports bring together multiple organisations, controlled areas and time-critical procedures. Where an assignment requires more than ordinary passenger or cargo processing, Sovereignty Logistics can coordinate with the relevant airport, airline and approved authority to establish a workable operational arrangement.

Services may include

  • Advance airport notification
  • Airline operations liaison
  • Terminal and cargo-area coordination
  • Escorted airside access requests
  • Airside induction coordination
  • Aircraft-side interface requests
  • Loading and unloading supervision
  • Connection and transfer planning
  • Disruption and missed-connection escalation

All restricted-area and aircraft-side access remains subject to the approval and control of the relevant airport, authority and operational stakeholders.
