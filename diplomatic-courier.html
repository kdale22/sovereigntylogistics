Our Services / Diplomatic Courier

Diplomatic Courier & Safe-Hand Services

Accountable custody from authorised acceptance to final handover.

Direct personal responsibility

Diplomatic courier assignments involve more than accompanying a consignment on a flight. They require clearly established authority, accurate instructions, appropriate documentation, controlled custody, dependable communications and a defined handover process.

Sovereignty Logistics works with the appointing mission or government authority to establish these requirements before the assignment begins.

What the service may include

  • Pre-assignment operational assessment
  • Written authority and instruction confirmation
  • Courier travel and route planning
  • Packaging, seal and identification checks
  • Continuous safe-hand accompaniment
  • Airport and airline coordination
  • Agreed status updates
  • Authorised handover and receipt
  • Completion and exception reporting

One responsible point of coordination

The appointing organisation receives a clearly identified operational contact and a defined process for instructions, approvals, updates and escalation. This reduces ambiguity and makes it easier to understand who is responsible at each stage.

Where an assignment involves a diplomatic bag or formally appointed diplomatic courier, Sovereignty Logistics operates only under the written appointment and instructions of the competent government authority or diplomatic mission. Operational arrangements are structured to respect applicable diplomatic protocols, including relevant Article 27 considerations where properly engaged.
