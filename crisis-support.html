Contingency & Crisis Support

Prepared for disruption, not surprised by it.

Structured escalation and recovery support

International movements can be affected by events outside any one organisation’s control. The difference lies in how quickly the issue is identified, who is notified, what authority exists and which approved alternatives are available.

Sovereignty Logistics incorporates contingency considerations into operational planning and maintains defined escalation pathways for assignments.

Areas considered

  • Flight cancellation or significant delay
  • Missed connections
  • Border or transit disruption
  • Airport-access issues
  • Unauthorised screening requests
  • Consignment damage or seal concern
  • Health or medical events
  • Fatigue and fitness-for-duty concerns
  • Communication failure
  • Security or civil disruption
  • Return, reroute or safe-holding requirements

Preparedness, not unrealistic promises.

The objective is not to promise that disruption will never occur. The objective is to ensure that foreseeable events have been considered and that decisions can be made through an authorised, documented process.

Discuss your operational requirement confidentially.

Provide high-level information only. Detailed instructions and protected documents are exchanged through an approved secure channel.
