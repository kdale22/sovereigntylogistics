Core value proposition

When the consignment matters, custody matters.

Official consignments cannot always be managed as ordinary freight. They may require defined responsibility, authorised personnel, documented handovers, controlled communications, specialised airport coordination and contingency arrangements that reflect the importance of the assignment.

Sovereignty Logistics brings these requirements together in one professionally managed service. We work from the client’s written instructions to plan the movement, identify responsibilities, coordinate relevant parties and maintain clear operational records.

Official consignments deserve more than ordinary freight handling.

We provide a clearly planned, accountable and professionally coordinated service from instruction through to final handover.
